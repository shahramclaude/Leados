# ADR-0256 — Lone-working chronology from recorded endpoints

- **Status:** Accepted; bounded extension to [ADR-0242](0242-lone-working-welfare-check-register.md).
- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Surface:** `#/lone-working?view=history`, the existing declaration and close-out dialogs, and two appended columns in the existing aggregate welfare CSV. Schema v208 and rule registrations are unchanged.

## Decision

A supervisor reconciling a handoff needs to tell a collision in the records from a gap in the evidence. [HSE lone-working guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/lone-working/employer/training-supervision-monitoring.htm) calls for a reliable means of confirming return, not assuming it. Use an exact interval constraint, as in [Timefold's no-overlapping-shifts constraint](https://docs.timefold.ai/timefold-solver/latest/constraints-and-score/score-calculation), but do not treat missing actual ends as schedule endpoints. The [SHEQSY finish and activity-history workflow](https://safetyculture.com/topics/lone-working/working-alone-procedure) sets the operational bar for a durable actual finish and reviewable source history. Native evaluation keeps the workspace offline and produces the exact conflicting records and instants; neither vendor was run, and the register does not match connected monitoring, dispatch or scheduling **[BENCHMARK-UNVERIFIED]**.

- Compare two records for one person once, independent of the reporting path, site label or line that declared them. Intervals are half-open at millisecond precision. A supported recorded close at the later start proves abutment; a zero-length closed span does not occupy time. No per-person rating and no sum of pair durations as time worked.
- Two supported recorded close spans can give an exact **recorded overlap**. Where a close is absent, contradicted by a later act or after review time, an intersection using the expected end is only a **window overlap**. If those windows do not intersect, separation is still **not proved** unless a supported close establishes it. These are findings about records, not evidence of physical whereabouts. A future start is flagged and not included in the historical pair comparison.
- An imported check-in or escalation after the recorded close stays in the ledger and finding list; neither it nor a future act is used by the welfare clock at the review instant. An act exactly at close is admissible. An imported close after review time does not end the clock early. The report distinguishes the counts of readable rows and findings from unknown source coverage. The queue pages through all findings within the existing ledger caps.
- A new open declaration is refused if it would overlap another window or cannot prove separation, in **both directions**, including retroactive entry before a later closed record. A close requires an explicit actual time and is refused before any accepted contact or escalation on that deployment. A correctly ordered actual close can be recorded even when it reveals a cross-record conflict; no evidence or other deployment is moved to make the register look clean. UTC form values and explicit offsets are normalized without local-time reinterpretation or sub-millisecond rounding.
- Use the existing home span and active declared additional lines. Frontline, mid-manager and director seats see in-reach rows; VP and the declared enterprise seat see scoped counts and no write affordances. The local undeclared operator retains the whole-register view. The four ledgers are report-only for declared seats; generic projection and aggregation cannot reconstruct the review. This is workflow disclosure, not authentication. The screen exports its own scoped reading. Only the total number of findings is eligible for CSV, and only when source coverage was not refused and findings cover at least the configured number of **distinct people**. No overlap times or source identifiers are exported.

## Consequences and limits

No derived finding is written. Contradictory but readable source acts survive reload. The loader still rejects unreadable rows and reports what it refused on that load; a later backup of the normalized workspace cannot attest to rows already discarded or omitted. The review is not a reconstruction of what was known on a historical day. There is no automatic finish, location inference, safety clearance, scheduling, SMS, or telemetry integration. An append-only sourced correction/reconciliation workflow and atomic entry of an already-ended deployment remain **In Progress**; do not fabricate a close to work around them.
