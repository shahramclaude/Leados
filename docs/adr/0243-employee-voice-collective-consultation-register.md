# ADR-0243 — Employee Voice & Collective Consultation Register: a declared body, an item with the period its instrument sets, advice, a reply, a decision — and the ordering between them printed, never repaired

- **Status:** accepted and built — Schema v197, route `#/voice`, `RULE-VOI-01` on the shared spine, CSV scope #184, demo seed, insights signals (VOI-01..04), focused suite 28/28.
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Deciders:** A director is told, after a change was signed, that the staff council says it was never really consulted. Nobody can reconstruct **whether the decision preceded the period the body was given** — because the workspace can store a ticked box called "consultation opens with employee representatives" and cannot store a consultation. This register exists to answer one question: **was the advice heard before the decision, and if not, how many days before the window closed was the decision taken?**
- **Schema:** **v197** (v196 → v197). Seven additive collections, all **empty at upgrade**: `voiceBodies`, `voiceMembers`, `voiceItems`, `voiceSittings`, `voiceResponses`, `voiceDecisions`, `voiceMarkers`. Four bounded conventions: `voiceAutoPropagate` (pause switch, defaulted ON), `voicePrivacyFloor` 3 [2–20], `voiceReplyDays` 10 [1–60], `voiceMinutesDueDays` 10 [1–60].
- **Surface:** `#/voice` — Board (what the register is carrying, the two claims reported apart), Items (subject, the instrument that set the period, its affected lines, what came back, whether it was answered, and whether the decision preceded the close), Sittings (attendance against the body's own quorum, the items heard, minutes and their reference), Lines (each line's holding position and its open duties), Method (the framework basis, the state vocabulary, and what the register refuses to do). Nine dialogs: declare a body, declare a representative, put an item, record a sitting, record minutes, record advice, answer the advice, record the decision, set conventions. ## Context

Consultation is the one employment duty whose **quality is decided by its order**. The EU information-and-consultation framework (Directive 2002/14/EC and the EWC recast 2009/38/EC) does not require agreement; it requires that the body be given relevant information **in good time** and be able to express a view **before the decision is taken** **[directive text; hands-on parity `[BENCHMARK-UNVERIFIED]`]**. Works-council and employee-relations case tooling in the category (works-council suites, ER case-management products) converges on the same four objects — a body with dated members, an agenda item with its documents, a period, a minute with the body's opinion — and then stores them as an unordered bag of meetings and attachments. The practitioner failure modes are record failures, not app failures:

1. **A ticked box stands in for a consultation.** The transition checklist says "consultation opened"; the tick survives the meeting being cancelled.
2. **The period is the software's default.** A "10 working days" constant applied to a shift change and to a redundancy programme is not the instrument's period, and the record cannot show which it was.
3. **Advice is treated as agreement.** A minute that says "representatives briefed; no objection recorded" is recorded as consent, and the departure nobody took is lost.
4. **Silence is read as approval.** The window closes, nobody from the body replies, and the change proceeds with the box green — the exact sequence that produces a collective grievance months later.
5. **The decision date is never compared with the close.** This is the central one. Almost no tool derives whether the decision preceded the period, so the only question an inquiry asks is the one the record cannot answer.

Verified before building: word-boundary searches of `app/` returned **zero** files for `information and consultation`, `collective consultation`, `works-council`, `employee forum`, `staff representative`, `trade union` and `joint consultative`; `works council` appeared twice, both in prose explaining that duties vary by jurisdiction, and `consultation` otherwise names the Diligence Ledger's "consulted before a decision" — a duty discharged by the decision-maker alone — or the transition checklist row above. `#/voice` was unregistered, `RULE-VOI-01` did not exist, and CSV scope #184 was unused. Wider semantic absence **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## Decision

**A body is declared against a written instrument; each item carries the period that instrument sets; advice, the organisation's written reply and the decision are three separate dated acts; and the reader derives the ordering between the decision and the close on every read, printing the exact days early or late. Nothing is repaired, nothing is inferred, and no representative is ever scored.**

1. **Nothing is inferred, and a tick is not a consultation.** A body needs a name, a kind from a closed list (`works-council`, `cooperation-committee`, `employee-forum`, `union-recognition`, `ad-hoc`), the **written instrument** that constitutes it (≥24 characters), the day it was constituted, an optional term end, and its own quorum. A representative needs a dated term and, when elected, **the day of the election** — an elected seat with no election day is refused. An item needs a subject, a **kind** (`consultation`, `negotiation`, `information`), a **written instrument reference**, **at least one affected reporting line**, and the **period in whole days (1–90) that instrument gives it** — there is no jurisdiction table, no statutory default and no software-chosen period anywhere in the file.

2. **The period and the close are arithmetic, and the days are exact.** `closesOn = openedOn + windowDays`. `daysRemaining` is whole days to the close, `adviceLateDays` is whole days past the close that advice arrived, `replyOverdueDays` is whole days past the declared reply day, and `earlyDays` is whole days a decision preceded the close. A span is floored, so the register never claims a day that has not finished; a decision **on** the closing day is in time and a decision one day early prints `1`.

3. **Fourteen item states, and silence is one of them.** `advice-owed`, `advice-lapsed` (the window closed with nothing on the record — **not** agreement), `advised`, `advised-late` (kept, with its exact late days), `advice-replied`, `reply-overdue`, `decided`, `decided-departing` (a decision that departs from the advice carries its own written reason), `decided-without-advice`, `implemented`, `notified` (an information item owes nothing back; the advice courses are `support`, `amend`, `oppose` and `no-view`, and `no-view` is explicitly not agreement), and three breaches: `breach-decided-before-advice`, `breach-implemented-before-decision`, `breach-body-lapsed` (an item put to a body after its declared term — the hands that advised were not constituted to advise).

4. **Breaches are kept, never repaired — and the door refuses to create them.** A hand-edited decision dated before the close survives the loader and prints `breach-decided-before-advice` with its exact early days; an implementation dated before its decision prints `breach-implemented-before-decision`. The commands cannot create either: a decision before the close is refused with its reason and the exact early days, an implementation before its decision is refused, and a decision on a departure course without advice is refused (`course-needs-advice`). The loader's job is to carry what the record says; the door's job is to stop the record gaining one it does not say.

5. **The joint seat decides, and one line cannot decide for two.** A consultation item names the reporting lines whose work changes. The seat answerable is the **joint apex** above them, computed through `matrixJointApex`. A holder of only one affected line recording the decision is refused (`not-apex`) — that is the matrix silently picking a winner between two managers. A person is counted once in every people figure; a body is not a reporting line and is never addressed as if it were a person.

6. **Six sitting states against the body's own rules.** `scheduled`, `minuted`, `minutes-owed`, `minutes-late`, `minutes-lapsed` (the declared day passed with no minute — a sitting nobody minuted cannot be shown to have advised anything), and `below-quorum` (fewer representatives attended than the body's own constitution requires; the register records the sitting and does not pretend the body transacted).

7. **`RULE-VOI-01` asks, once, pausably, and only the hand that can act.** Four conditions: `advice-lapsed`, `reply-overdue`, `breach`, and `minutes-lapsed`. One ask **per obligation** — per item or sitting and condition — with an opaque signature `voi1:<kind>:<id>` that lives only in the `issues-obligation` link, addressed to the joint apex above the affected lines. Idempotent per (signature, addressee), self-closing when the condition clears, capped at **4** new asks per commit with the remainder **counted as deferred**, governed by `voiceAutoPropagate` on every path including the manual scan, and never re-entered from an automation event. Where the affected lines share **no manager who holds both** — the only common seat is the synthetic enterprise root — the rule writes an **identity-free marker** and asks nobody: filing an obligation to a node that holds no line would be an obligation to nobody.

8. **Reading is open; the reader withholds names above rank 3.** Every person the body represents may see what was put to it and what it advised, so the read right is rank 1. Above rank 3 the reader returns the aggregate level: subjects, representative names and written advice are withheld with their reason — what a named body advised about a named change is collective material belonging to the people in it, not executive reading. Writing is a rank 2–5 leader act narrowed inside the action: an item is opened only against a line this seat holds, and a decision only by the joint apex above the affected lines.

9. **Nobody is scored.** The register counts items, days, sittings and advice. It never prints a per-member attendance rate, a "cooperation index", a ranking of bodies or a named comparison. A voice channel that rates the people using it is a channel that closes.

10. **The export is a count, never a person or a body.** CSV #184 emits one summary row plus one row per printed state and per sitting state. No person id, no name, no body name, no subject, no instrument reference, no advice note, no reply note and no day crosses that boundary. A count below the privacy floor exports **blank with its reason**, never `0`; `complete` is separate from the counts and from `balances` (`statesReconcile`, `figuresComplete`), and the two claims are never collapsed into one.

## Consequences

- A workspace upgraded to v197 gains a register that is **honestly empty**, with its four conventions declared and its first body refused until somebody writes the instrument the duty rests on.
- The register's most valuable output is the **ordering**: `breach-decided-before-advice` and `advised-late` are facts almost no product in the category prints, and both are exactly what a collective-grievance or inquiry file asks for first.
- The demo seed exercises the whole vocabulary on first load: one body with four dated representatives (one departed), five items across all three kinds, three sittings (one below quorum, one with no minutes), three pieces of advice (one late, one answered, one unanswered past the reply day), two decisions (one departing from advice and implemented, one following advice), and fresh states `advice-owed`, `advice-lapsed` and `decided-without-advice` — totals `{items: 5, consultations: 3, negotiations: 1, information: 1, advisedN: 1, advisedLateN: 1, adviceLapsedN: 1, repliedN: 2, replyOverdueN: 1, decisionsN: 2, departedN: 1, implementedN: 1, sittingsN: 3, belowQuorumN: 1, minutesLapsedN: 1, breachesN: 0, ownersN: 3}`, `statesReconcile: true`, `figuresComplete: true`.
- **Deliberately not built (scoped out this session, logged honestly):** no statutory jurisdiction table (the period is always the instrument's, so the register cannot become a compliance checker for a law it does not cite); no automatic election records; no document storage — an item holds declared document labels and dates, not files; no per-member attendance analytics; no redundancy/collective-consultation special case.
- **Method figure:** the Method tab prints its framework basis and engine version as text. No explanatory image was produced this session; the figure is cosmetic, not evidentiary, and is reported as missing rather than faked.
- **Benchmark `[BENCHMARK-UNVERIFIED]`:** the bar is taken from the Directive 2002/14/EC and 2009/38/EC texts, Hirschman (1970) on voice and exit, and published descriptions of works-council and ER case-management suites. **Cleared natively:** a declared body with dated members, an item with its documents, a period, a minute and a recorded opinion, a decision. **Exceeds:** the period is the instrument's own and never a default; the ordering between decision and close is derived and printed with exact days; silence is a printed state, not consent; a departure from advice is its own record with a reason; the organisation's written reply is a first-class dated act with its own overdue state; the ask goes to the joint apex and never to a representative. No vendor environment was run and no hands-on parity is claimed.
