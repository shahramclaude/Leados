# ADR-0259 — Versioned minimum-cash covenant receipts

**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** Accepted for canonical source · **Surface:** `#/cash-covenant` · **Storage:** v210 → v211.

## Decision

A treasury director reviewing quarter-end liquidity should not have to rerun yesterday’s spreadsheet or ask which revision the VP saw. A budget envelope says what can be spent; it cannot evidence a lender’s minimum cash test. Bring the existing minimum-cash workpaper into the workspace, using financial-control reconciliation: agree an independently sourced account count and signed book balance **before** applying the executed clause’s eligibility. Director and VP can record; declared CEO review is read-only. Frontline and mid-manager seats do not get lender or balance detail. Neither a review nor a recorded receipt constitutes compliance, default, a waiver, or authority to sign or draw.

## Evidence and calculation

- One series identifies one stable facility code, contractual local date label and currency. No home/project copy gets a separate series. Declare the actual cutoff as a UTC instant no later than capture; its UTC calendar date can be one day either side of the contractual date to accommodate timezone rollovers. The declaration does not prove that a balance was observed at that instant. Every extract supplies the effective clause/amendment reference and minimum as of that test, a workpaper version reference, the count and signed amount before eligibility, and a distinct book control reference. Referencing another document is not proof of independence. Account, facility and source references are not credentials or full account numbers.
- The existing TSV command and the workspace use the same versioned `BigInt` kernel in `minimumCash.js`. Identical copies count once; conflicting copies refuse the file. The workspace also refuses case-ambiguous account identities and makes facility codes case-insensitive for series uniqueness. Print the exact distinct-account rows, their original TSV line numbers, and the three classification subtotals. Reconcile the row sum and subtotals separately from count agreement, book-total agreement and eligibility completeness. No row evidence, unresolved eligibility or a disagreement with either control withholds headroom; a readable withheld workpaper can still be recorded as evidence. A supported reading is `included signed balances − minimum`, compared inclusively with zero, never rounded. These checks do not prove account membership, authenticity, or legal interpretation. Neither FX, eligibility caps, partial balances, borrowing capacity nor aggregation across test dates is inferred.
- Bring the complete source to preview; the preview writes nothing. Record only canonical source data, the UTC capture instant, declared recording seat, and method version, never a caller-supplied result. Each correction names the current version, gives a new source version and a reason, and resubmits a complete extract for the same perimeter and stewards. A stale revision or a changed recording seat requires a new preview. Earlier receipts are reopenable by exact revision link and labelled superseded; latest means latest **locally recorded**, not current agreement or lender evidence. Imports check the entire ledger, including duplicate IDs/perimeters, chronological linkage, and method. An invalid ledger is withheld, with its material retained in the full backup; no earlier version is silently promoted because a newer one could not be loaded. This is an append-only product workflow, not tamper-proof storage: an externally edited or replaced backup can omit or falsify evidence.

## Boundaries and costs

- Read and write reach is recomputed on the existing active reporting graph; every declared steward must hold a leadership seat and be in reach. Reaching one of several stewards is insufficient; multiple paths never duplicate a steward. A change to that set requires a separate governance design, not a correction. The undeclared local operator may inspect the ledger for recovery but cannot record. These are workflow seats in one browser-owned workspace, not multi-user authentication.
- The collection starts empty. Limits: 64 KiB UTF-8 per extract, 512 KiB total TSV, 120 tests, 12 revisions per test and 8 stewards per test. The limits refuse without truncation. Save and the opaque audit act use the existing transaction and vault; if browser storage fails the workpaper is identified as memory-only. Use one editing tab; the workspace does not synchronize across browsers or reconcile concurrent tabs. Full backups retain source evidence, including ordinary backups with unrelated redaction options; do not use them as a scoped handoff. No generic projection, grant, release, CSV, or automated notification consumes this report-only ledger. No demo balances were invented.

## Benchmark and remainder

[2](https://finquery.com/debt-management-software/) describes FinQuery’s AI-assisted agreement intake with human review, timestamped audit trails and continuing cash balance and DSCR tests. That is the specialist workflow bar. Native deterministic reconciliation is appropriate for exact submitted evidence; cash facts must not be inferred by a model. This slice supplies dated clause snapshots, reproducible exact minor-unit receipts, input refusals and traceable revisions. It does not do AI extraction, continuous ingestion, comparative vendor execution or lender approval. **[BENCHMARK-UNVERIFIED]** for same-input and full-workflow parity.

**In Progress, not implemented:** reusable effective-dated facility terms and amendment applicability across dates, ratio definitions and add-backs, notice and cure clocks, and governed waiver or lender certification. No generated visual, onboarding or communication asset is part of the data workpaper.
