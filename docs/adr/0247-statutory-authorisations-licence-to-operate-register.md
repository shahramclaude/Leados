# ADR-0247 — Statutory Authorisation & Licence-to-Operate Register: which power to operate is held, on what written instrument, whether it is in force, which conditions are evidenced, and what the regulator has already ordered

- **Status:** accepted and built — Schema v201, route `#/authorisations`, `RULE-LTO-01` on the shared spine (**160/160**), CSV scope #188, demo seed, insights signals (LTO-01..05), focused suite **26/26**.
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Deciders:** The tank farm holds an environmental permit, a hazardous-substances consent and a tanker-loading authorisation. The permit expired three days ago; the renewal was filed yesterday and nobody has decided it. A prohibition notice has stood against night loading for a fortnight. Two of the permit's seven conditions have no evidence recorded since summer. Every one of those facts is answerable from a filing cabinet after the inspector arrives, and **none of them was answerable from the workspace**, which could store a delegation, a stop-work hold, a permit to work and a regulatory undertaking — and could not store the instrument that says the site is allowed to operate at all. This register exists to answer five questions the record could not previously ask: **what power to operate is held, on what written instrument, is it in force at this instant, which of its conditions are evidenced, and what has the regulator already ordered.**
- **Schema:** **v201** (v200 → v201). Six additive collections, all **empty at upgrade**: `ltoAuthorisations`, `ltoConditions`, `ltoEvidence`, `ltoActions`, `ltoRenewals`, `ltoMarkers`. No authority, condition, evidence row, regulator action or renewal is inferred from anything an upgraded workspace already holds. Three bounded conventions: `ltoAutoPropagate` (pause switch, defaulted ON), `ltoPrivacyFloor` 3 [2–20], `ltoHorizonDays` 90 [1–365]. **There is deliberately no default renewal lead, no default condition window and no default validity**: each belongs to the instrument that imposes it, and a row that declares none is refused rather than handed a house value.

## Context

Every site that handles fuel, food, waste, water, vehicles or hazardous substances operates under written authorities granted by an external body: an environmental permit, a hazardous-substances consent, an operating licence, an abstraction licence, an approval, a registration. Those instruments carry conditions, they expire, they are renewed on a lead time the instrument itself sets, and the regulator can suspend, revoke or restrict them by notice. The failure modes are consistent and all of them are record failures:

1. **The register stores a typed status.** `Active`, `Expiring Soon`, `Expired` are columns somebody edits. The moment an import, a clock change or a careless edit desynchronises them, the register asserts a state the facts no longer support.
2. **A submission is mistaken for a grant.** A renewal filed and acknowledged is entered as "renewed". The authority is still the old one, still expiring, and nobody decides it because the row says it is fine.
3. **A closure is recorded against an act that cannot close.** Somebody records "lifted" against a revocation and the screen prints a live authority from a terminal act.
4. **The condition is treated as met because the licence exists.** A permit with seven conditions and one inspection certificate is printed as compliant, because the condition list is prose nobody reads against evidence.
5. **The notice is filed as correspondence.** A prohibition notice sits in a document store while the activity it prohibits continues, and no surface multiplies the licence state by the notice state.
6. **A lapse is reported as an allegation.** "Expired" becomes "operating without a permit" in a report, when the record can only prove the authority lapsed — not what anybody did next.

The vendor bar is the obligation/consent half of the GRC and licence-management category. Benchmarked against fetched vendor descriptions on 2026-11-17: **Effivity** (statutory/regulatory obligation register, permit conditions tracked against expiry, renewal raised early, conditions broken out and monitored individually, violation notices logged and investigated), **ExpiryEdge** (one record per licence per site per state, renewal worked back from the prerequisite, filed-but-unverified stays open, "track, alert, renew, verify, re-arm"), **InfoDeck** (permit register field set, 90/60/30/7-day reminder ladder, escalation when renewals stall, audit evidence), and the **GiTnux** landscape (Archer IRM, MetricStream, LogicGate, OneTrust, Diligent, NAVEX One) for the category comparison. Parity is stated as **[vendor documentation, hands-on parity UNVERIFIED]** — no vendor environment was run.

**The positive gap claim, and what was rejected as already covered.** The workspace could already store: permission to do a job (permit to work, ADR-0246), an obligation arising from a regulator's action (regulatory undertaking, ADR-0234), a stop-work hold (ADR-0224), the boundary of what is insourced (ADR-0229), an incident and its notification clocks (ADR-0240), a mutual-aid promise (ADR-0244), and a delegated duty (the spine). Those were each examined and rejected as **not** the gap: a permit authorises work, not the power to operate the site; an undertaking is a promise made *to* a regulator, not an instrument granted *by* one; a stop-work hold is an internal freeze, not a suspension of a consent; a sourcing case is a commercial boundary. None of them stores an issuing body, an instrument reference, a declared expiry, a renewal window, a declared condition with its own evidence window, or a regulator's suspension, revocation, prohibition or improvement notice against an activity. **Nineteen search terms across `app/`, 259 ADRs, `ROADMAP.md` and the session log returned zero hits for that record.** The gap is real and the claim is written into `authorisationRegistry.js`'s header so the hunt is auditable.

## Decision

Build the register as one reader over six append-only ledgers, with the state **derived on every read** and **never stored**.

**1. The instrument declares; the reader cannot invent.** An authority names its site, activity, issuing body, a **written instrument reference**, a grant instant, a declared expiry, a renewal lead in whole days, and the people accountable for it. A condition names a key unique on that authority, the instrument's own wording, and a re-evidence window in whole days (0 means "no window", stored as declared). Evidence names the condition, a written reference and the instant it was taken. A regulator action names its kind, the written basis, the instant served and the instant effective, and — for an improvement notice — the compliance day its own instrument set. A renewal names the submission instant and basis; a decision names the decision, the instant and the hand. **Anything unreadable is dropped with a counted warning and never repaired**, including: no instrument, a reversed span, a fractional lead, a partially readable holder list, a duplicate condition key on one authority, a second evidence row at the same instant, a half-closed action, an improvement notice with no compliance day, a half-decided renewal, a grant that would *shorten* validity, and a refusal carrying an expiry.

**2. Nine states, nine different facts.** `in-force`, `expiring` (inside the renewal lead the declaration itself set, with no renewal submitted), `lapsed`, `lapsed-awaiting-decision` (lapsed with a renewal submitted and undecided), `not-yet-granted` (granted in the future), `suspended`, `revoked`, `prohibited` (in force, but a prohibition notice stands against the activity), and `renewal-in-flight` (in force, inside or outside the window, with an undecided renewal). A suspension outranks a lapse it carries; a prohibition outranks the in-force state it sits on; a revocation outranks everything and says so. The headline never hides the flags underneath: the row prints the improvement notices, the ignored close, the unevidenced conditions and the renewal facts separately.

**3. Two clocks, never conflated.** The **effective expiry** is the declared expiry, raised by every *granted* renewal's own expiry. The **renewal window opens** at the effective expiry minus the authority's own declared lead. A submission moves neither; a refusal moves neither; only a granted decision moves the expiry, and it is refused at the door if it would move it backwards. Whole days are UTC calendar days; elapsed minutes are floored from the expiry instant; nothing is rounded into a friendlier number.

**4. A closure recorded against a terminal act is kept and printed.** A revocation carrying `closedAt`/`closeKind`/`closeBasisRef`/`closedById` is a record of somebody believing they lifted a revocation. Deleting the row would erase the revocation; deleting the fields would erase the belief. The loader keeps both (`ignoredClose`) and the reader prints that the close has **no effect**. It survives re-normalisation, because the flag and the payload are carried forward when the raw fields are already null — the defect this rule exists to prevent.

**5. A condition is current, stale against its own window, or unevidenced.** The latest dated evidence decides. `maxAgeDays: 0` means no window, not "always stale". A live licence is not evidence that its conditions are met, and the register never marks one compliant on anybody's behalf.

**6. A lapse proves the lapse and nothing else.** Where an authority is past its effective expiry with no suspension or revocation recorded, the row says `noStopRecorded` and the method panel says plainly that the register **cannot prove the activity stopped**. That sentence is a refusal to convert a record gap into an allegation.

**7. The joint apex, never a chosen manager.** Every accountable hand is resolved across **every active line** by the one matrix engine; the ask goes to `matrixJointApex` above all of them, because a consent held across two lines cannot be answered by one line for the other. A hand off the graph is printed as an orphan and coverage is not certified (`figuresComplete` false, with reasons). The register never silently picks a manager for an ambiguous apex.

**8. `RULE-LTO-01` files five conditions, and only five.** `lapsed` (including lapsed-awaiting-decision), `prohibition`, `improvement-overdue`, `conditions` (unevidenced or stale), `expiring` (inside the lead with nothing submitted). One ask per (opaque `lto1:<kind>:<authorityId>` signature, addressee), at most four new asks per commit with the remainder **counted deferred**, self-closing when the condition clears or the addressee moves, never re-filing an ask a human closed or returned, identity-free marker where no seat resolves, and pause-governed on every path including the explicit scan. The ask text names no site, activity, issuing body, instrument, notice or person: a delegation is readable above the addressee, and the signature lives only in the typed `issues-obligation` link.

**9. Disclosure follows the duty, not a blanket rank.** Above rank 3 the register prints counts and withholds accountable hands; the seat that holds a person, sits above them or is them can read that authority's rows. The method panel states the exact rule so a reader knows what is being withheld and why.

**10. The export is aggregate and identity-free, and floors small counts.** CSV #188 carries the summary row, one row per printed authority state and one row per printed condition state. No site, activity, issuing body, instrument reference, notice, person, manager or instant crosses the boundary: *which site has lost which consent* is exactly the disclosure that makes an export dangerous. A per-state count below `ltoPrivacyFloor` is exported **BLANK with its reason**, never as a small number; a computed zero is exported as zero; `complete` is printed beside the figures.

## Consequences

- The workspace can now answer, at an instant, which powers to operate are held, whether each is in force, which are inside a renewal window and undecided, which conditions have no evidence, which notices stand and which improvement notices are overdue — and it derives every one of those from append-only declarations rather than a status column.
- A renewal submission no longer looks like a renewal: `renewal-in-flight` is a distinct state and the expiry does not move until a decision is recorded.
- A revocation cannot be erased by an attempted lift, and a notice cannot be hidden by an in-force licence; both contradictions are printed rather than resolved.
- The spine gains its 160th rule and the pause register gains its switch, with the reach claim derived from source. **Two real census defects were found and fixed while wiring it:** six reach switches had no rule mapping (so the census under-counted the matrix reach of six engines), and `workStopRules.js` / `mgrHandoverRules.js` read their switches through constants, so the source-derived reach proof could not see them. Four further rules that compute an ask through the reporting graph (`assurance`, `mutual aid`, `personal dealing`, `priority arbitration`) were gated by switches missing from the reach list entirely; they are now claimed. `pause-permits.test.mjs`'s first reach assertion is green; its second (the declared-settings census) remains **red on an inherited defect unrelated to this ADR**: fourteen reach switches are absent from `DEFAULT_SETTINGS`, so the census cannot count them. That is reported, not repaired here, because adding fourteen default conventions is a workspace-wide schema decision with its own ADR.
- **Six insights domains were being silently dropped from the roll-up** (`Incident command`, `Personal dealing`, `Permit to work`, `Mutual aid` — eleven findings on the demo — plus this register's own five). Found because the new signals made the roll-up test fail by exactly the missing counts; the buckets are now listed and `domain.test.mjs` is green.
- A method diagram asset (`app/assets/statutory-authorisation-method.jpg`) is **not produced**: no asset-generation tool is available in this conversation (`higgsfield` is unsupported). The view references it with alt text in the same way as the three preceding registers; no placeholder and no fabricated image was substituted, and the blocker is recorded here.
