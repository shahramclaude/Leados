## Context

A team spread over time zones pays an uneven price for collaboration. The 21:30 bridge is a mid-afternoon call for the chair and a middle-of-the-night call for someone else, but no shipped module measures that asymmetry — and the tools that touch the space (Viva Insights, Clockwise, Reclaim) score *people* from *calendar observation*, which LeadOS will not do. Meanwhile the matrix adds a trap the hierarchy does not have: when a chair convenes a sitting for someone held on two declared lines, "which line convened this?" has no honest single answer, and a naive roll-up double-counts the burden into both lines.

Verified before building: no `dwe*` vocabulary existed in `app/` or `tests/`; nothing in `app/` reads a person's time zone from any store; `workRestTime` stores shifts with zones but no *window* concept and no sitting ledger.

## Decision

**A window is a written-source declaration per person. A sitting is a declared event. Burden is exact minutes placed in each attendee's own local day. The verdict and the coverage claim are two separate printed facts.**

1. **Windows are declared, sourced and superseded, never inferred.** A window is one person's IANA zone and an HH:MM span of at most 900 minutes on their *local* day, carried with a non-empty written source (the loader drops an unsourced window and counts the drop). Declaring a new window supersedes the active one in the open — both stay in the ledger; two active windows for one person are *kept* and refuse on read rather than silently latest-wins. Withdrawal is final and recorded.
2. **Sittings declare who met, when, and under whose clock.** A sitting carries day, HH:MM start, minutes 1–480, a zone, up to 40 attendees all of whom must be on the roster (one off-roster attendee refuses the whole sitting — never a partial attribution), an optional chair, and for a boundary that exists twice (DST fold) a declared first/second occurrence; an ambiguous start is refused until it names its occurrence.
3. **Placement is exact and per-attendee.** The burden of a sitting on a person is the integer minutes that fall *outside that person's declared window* on *that person's local day*, resolved through the DST engine: a nonexistent local start (spring gap) refuses the reading; a fold resolves to the declared occurrence; a window boundary that falls inside a gap refuses the reading. The same sitting therefore prints different — both correct — outside minutes for attendees in different zones. A person with no readable window is **unknown, not zero**, and the register says so.
4. **Attribution follows the one matrix engine, with named buckets for the ties.** Attendance lands in exactly one bucket per attendee-sitting: the chair's declared line when the chair holds exactly one active line to that attendee (and never via a second engine); **self** when the attendee convenes their own sitting; **off-graph chair** when the chair is on the roster but holds no active line to the attendee (e.g. a grand-manager chairing a skip-level); and named no-declared-line buckets for anything a line cannot honestly claim. A line's roll-up is the exact sum of the attendances attributed to it — no double-count, no drop, no invented allocation.
5. **Equity is a share verdict above declared floors, and it is refused below them.** With at least `dweEquityMinPeople` readable people, `dweEquityMinSittings` valid sittings and `dweEquityMinOutsideMinutes` placed burden, the verdict compares the top member's share of placed burden against the even share 1/n; 2× above even reads **concentrated**, otherwise **distributed**. Below any floor the verdict is refused *with its reason* — never rendered as reassuring. The floors are deliberately two separate claims: the **coverage claim** (counts reconcile, every printed total equals the sum of its printed rows) and the **verdict** (measured only) are reported as distinct facts.
6. **`RULE-DWE-01` asks the joint apex, once, pausably.** When a readable person's trailing-window outside minutes exceed `dweOutsideMinutesCeiling`, one opaque ask is delegated to `matrixJointApex(own ∪ chair managers)` — the person carrying the burden is never asked about their own burden. Signature `dwe1:over-ceiling:<personId>:<windowFrom>`; identity-free observations (`dwe1|unreadable|…`, `dwe1|no-apex|…`); capped at four per commit; idempotent on re-run; self-closing when the burden drops below the ceiling; fully governed by `dweAutoPropagate` and the pause-permit registry.
7. **The CSV is aggregate and identity-free.** Scope #179 prints counts and totals only: blank with a reason where a figure cannot be computed, never zero-as-reassurance, no person names, no window times.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A — infer windows from calendar/mail density (the "when do they actually work" tool behaviour).** Rejected: cadence stores no wall-clock or zone data, and LeadOS does not score people from observation. The register *declares* meetings and *declares* windows; what it cannot support it refuses, printed.
- **Option B — a single workspace-level "collaboration hours" overlay.** Rejected: it would place every sitting in one zone, which is exactly the asymmetry the module exists to measure, and it cannot answer per-person burden at all.
- **Option C — score people (an "always-on" leaderboard).** Rejected outright: the register measures *burden* and asks *seats*, never rates persons.

## Consequences

- **Easier:** a leader can now answer "who is always on the late call, and is it shared?" from declared facts with exact minute placement; the matrix traps (two lines, skip-level chairs, off-roster attendees) all land in named, counted buckets; `RULE-DWE-01` reaches the right seat through the one matrix engine.
- **Harder / debt:** windows and sittings are *declarations* — a stale window under-reports burden until someone updates it, and the register prints its as-of day so a reader can judge that; the DST engine's refusal on gap boundaries means some readings are unavailable by design; the equity verdict deliberately stays silent below the floors, which can look like "no finding" to a skimming reader (the refusal reason is printed, and DWE signals stay quiet below floors too).
- **Revisit when:** a team-level (not workspace-level) equity verdict is asked for; or when calendar-adjacent *declared* feeds (an imported sitting export) justify a bulk sitting door. Concrete trigger: a leader asks for per-team verdicts in the same register.

**Honest repairs made during the TDD loop (reader behaviour changed, then re-verified):** (1) person rows are built from *attendees of valid sittings*, not the full roster — a roster member who never attended gets no invented zero-filled row; (2) line attribution consumes the reader's line *records* rather than distinct-manager id sets, so a chair holding two declared lines of the same attendee cannot attribute by array accident — the two-claims-apart and one-person-once rules are asserted on the real behaviour.
