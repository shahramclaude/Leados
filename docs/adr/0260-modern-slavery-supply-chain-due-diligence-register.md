# ADR-0260 — Modern Slavery & Supply-Chain Due-Diligence Statement Register

**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** Accepted for canonical source · **Surface:** `#/modern-slavery` · **Storage:** v211 → v212.

## Decision

A CEO or board reviewing a modern slavery statement should not have to trust a headline. The statutory regimes (UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 s.54; Australian Modern Slavery Act 2018 s.16) ask six questions — structure, policies, due diligence, risk assessment and management, effectiveness, training — and most published statements answer all six with one reassuring word. This register refuses that collapse: **each element is its own evidenced-or-missing verdict derived from the dated record beneath it**, and **the claim about identified instances is derived from the evidence and never typed** — where no assessment supports any claim the register prints `refused`, because "no instances identified" is a finding, and a finding needs evidence. An identified instance owes a remediation act that closes only when it is complete **with its completed day and evidence reference**; a plan is not a remedy. Primary altitude **CEO / board with director / VP execution** × **single-chain and matrix** (hands on a statement routinely sit on different lines, so every ask resolves the joint apex above ALL of them). The real duty: a named hand declares the publication deadline with its written basis, and the register holds the organisation to that declared window.

## Evidence and calculation

- **The deadline is declared, never computed.** A statement without a declared publication deadline prints `deadline-undeclared` — the register holds no corporate calendar and will never infer a window from a financial-year end. Each deadline carries the written basis that sets its window (e.g. MSA 2015 s.54(6), six months from the day after the financial year end). One deadline, one approval, one publication per statement; a second is refused. The approval (minute/written authority reference) and the publication (published day + live reference) are separate dated acts — approval is a fact and publication is another.
- **Six derived states, six different facts:** `deadline-undeclared`, `overdue-unpublished` (the statutory window has already closed with nothing published), `awaiting-publication`, `approved-unpublished`, `published-with-gaps`, `published`. A published statement its record does not support is refined to `published-with-gaps`; an unpublished one keeps its own state. Timeliness is derived by comparing the published day with the declared deadline day — on time or late with the exact day count; a late publication is never re-dated.
- **The supplier population is declared** (supplier reference, name, tier 1–5 as declared, country, category) with 1–4 responsible hands; unassessed suppliers are **printed**, never silently absent from a statement's coverage. Assessments bind (statement, supplier) with a closed kind vocabulary (`audit` `questionnaire` `site-visit` `certification` `self-assessment` `desktop-review` `worker-interview`), a closed outcome (`no-issues` `issues-found` `unable-to-assess` `not-applicable`), an assessor reference and an evidence reference. Findings bind an assessment with kind (`risk` `nonconformance` `instance`) × severity (`minor` `material` `severe`) — a risk indicator is never printed as an instance, and the vocabulary refuses to blur them. Remediation binds a finding with a closed kind vocabulary; `complete` requires the completed day AND the evidence reference, and the derived open count is printed from the rows, never stored.
- **The element matrix** (structure / policies / due diligence / risk management / effectiveness / training) prints each element's count and its own reason where missing — including an adverse assessment outcome with no finding beneath it as a risk-management gap. The four derived claims (`none-identified` `risks-only` `instances-identified` `refused`) are computed from assessments and findings only; a claim smuggled onto a statement row is ignored. The reader's totals re-add to their rows (`statesReconcile` etc. are printed), and `figuresComplete` is separate from `balances`.
- **RULE-MSA-01** (spine #168, `msaAutoPropagate`) files at most one dated ask per condition per joint seat, capped at 4 per commit with the remainder counted: `deadline-undeclared`, `overdue-unpublished`, `publication-due` (inside the declared warning horizon), `content-gap` (an approved/published statement its record does not support), `remediation-open`. Every ask goes to the **lowest joint apex above every line holding a responsible hand** — never to one line on another's behalf — and where nobody can be asked the rule writes ONE identity-free `msaMarkers` observation and substitutes nobody. Ask text is deliberately opaque (a due-diligence schedule names suppliers and findings about workers, and a delegation is readable above the addressee); the `msa1:<kind>:<subjectId>` signature lives only in the typed `issues-obligation` link. Asks are self-closing when the condition clears and answered-never-refiled; pause-governed on every path, including the explicit scan.

## Boundaries and costs

- Ten collections, all empty at upgrade (`msaStatements`, `msaDeadlines`, `msaApprovals`, `msaPublications`, `msaSuppliers`, `msaAssessments`, `msaFindings`, `msaRemediations`, `msaTraining`, `msaMarkers`) + four bounded conventions (`msaAutoPropagate` defaulted ON, `msaPrivacyFloor` 3, `msaWarnHorizonDays` 90, `msaAssessmentCycleDays` 365). **Nothing is inferred** from a sourcing case, a contract, a permit, a provision or a published document: manufacturing a statement at upgrade would put a statutory duty on the record no board accepted. Loaders are fail-closed (a row that cannot be read exactly is dropped with one counted aggregate warning; a child row naming no parent is dropped; an off-roster responsible hand is KEPT and printed as an orphan — deleting it would erase a live duty); the door refuses every write the loader would refuse on reload (S327 invariant) — including a statement with no written basis, which the loader now also drops.
- Read/write reach is recomputed on the live reporting graph (`read-modern-slavery` / `manage-modern-slavery`, rank 3–5, `PORTFOLIO_ROUTES` + collection policies + disclosure manifest `modern-slavery` all at the same floor). Above rank 4 the reader prints counts and withholds responsible hands and supplier detail; the CSV (#196 `modern-slavery-due-diligence`) is aggregate, identity-free and floored — counts and published-vocabulary keys only, with a `blank_reason` where the floor withholds a proportion. Seats are workflow declarations in one browser-owned workspace, not authentication. The register scores, ranks and rates **nothing and nobody**: a supplier risk score is exactly the fake precision this build refuses.
- **Asset provenance (mandate):** Higgsfield is unavailable in this environment (S323–S330). The method illustration `app/assets/modern-slavery-method.jpg` was generated with the built-in Arena `generate_image` tool (no text, no logos, workers rendered as respectful silhouettes under a protective boundary) and is labelled illustrative — it is not operational evidence. ## Benchmark and remainder

The specialist bar is the Sedex/SMETA ecosystem (audit sharing, SAQ and risk-assessment tooling feeding UK MSA s.54 statements, 65,000+ member businesses [1](https://www.sedex.com/)) and EcoVadis (questionnaire-based supplier evidence, labour & human-rights scorecards, corrective-action tracking) with Assent and Sourcemap covering multi-tier mapping and forced-labour/UFLPA programmes [2](https://www.riskwatch.com/top-10-compliance-management-software-for-supply-chain/) [3](https://zipdo.co/best/supply-chain-compliance-software/). What those platforms do **not** do is derive the statement's own claims from its evidence chain: they collect data and score suppliers; the claim "no instances identified" is asserted by a human and published. This register's bar is the opposite discipline — the claim is DERIVED and REFUSED where nothing supports it, every element carries its own evidenced-or-missing verdict with its reason, and an instance stays open until a remediation act is evidenced. Primary-vendor feature parity and same-input trials are **[BENCHMARK-UNVERIFIED]** (this sandbox reached only secondary comparison roundups; the Sedex feature URL 404'd).

**In Progress / unbuilt (logged in ROADMAP.md):** sanctions, export-licence and denied-party screening (a supplier schedule with no denied-party check is a schedule with a hole in it); recurring contractual obligations across the statement cycle; a supplier risk-scoring engine is deliberately refused rather than unbuilt; legal hold; gifts & hospitality; DSAR. None of this register is legal advice, a compliance certification, or a statement that any published claim is true; it is the record that lets a board see what its statement can and cannot prove.
