## Context

The enterprise promised something to somebody **outside itself**. An undertaking was given to a regulator; a licence condition was accepted; the board committed to a remedy; a management representation was signed over a declared scope; a sub-certification went up to a parent; a covenant was signed with a lender. The promise is real, it is dated, and it is enforceable against the entity.

Then three things happen in most organisations, and not one of them was visible anywhere in the workspace:

1. **the work is done and nobody outside is told.** The fix ships; the regulator, the board, the customer hears nothing. In the instruments that matter the promise is not discharged by doing the work — it is discharged by doing it **and reporting it**. The gap between "we did it" and "we told them" is precisely where this duty fails;
2. **the person who did the work grades it.** A function reports its own deliverable as met. Nobody independent on the reporting graph ever tested it. A remediation that closes on its deliverer's word is a claim, not a discharge; and
3. **the day passes quietly.** The due date goes by with no report on the record, and the organisation discovers it when the supervisor writes.

The repository had **no trace of the duty**. Word-boundary searches over `app/` returned **zero** hits for `undertaking`, `line of representation`, `management representation`, `licence condition`, `license condition`, `board commitment`, `board directive`, `counterparty report` and `closing report`. The adjacency check confirms the hole: ADR-0232 starts at a **failure found** and works inward (a finding, an owner, an independent test); ADR-0233 holds a **joiner's first decision window**; ADR-0219/0226 hold **declared risk retention**; the board room adjudicates **claims about the entity's own state**; the stakeholders module names an `external-regulator` as a party but records no promise to them. Nothing held the **outward promise** — what was promised, to whom, under which instrument, over what scope, on which lines, and whether the counterparty was ever told it was met.

## Decision

**A promise is declared, not inferred.** An executive records: counterparty (named, ≥ 3 chars), counterparty kind (`regulator | board | customer | lender | certifier | authority`), instrument (`licence-condition | regulatory-undertaking | board-resolution | management-representation | sub-certification | contractual-covenant`), instrument reference, declared scope (≥ 24 chars — what it covers *and what it does not*), due day, and one to six **obligation lines**, each with its own deliverable and day and its holder where one is already known. A due day in the past is refused; one more than 3,650 days out is refused as not a dated commitment. The declared identity — counterparty, kind, instrument, scope, due day — is **immutable**: a correction is a new undertaking.

**The matrix is the point.** A licence condition does not land on one department: it lands on operations, legal and finance at once, held by three different people with different managers. So an undertaking carries one or more obligation lines, each with its **own named holder** — named by a human, never inferred from org position, tenure or the reporting graph (`assign`). A line may be declared **without** a holder; it then reads `unassigned` and the register prints `line-unassigned` rather than guessing a name, and `figuresComplete` is false while any line carries no holder. The seat answerable for the **whole** promise is the **joint apex above every accountable line's manager**, computed with `matrixJointApex` and never chosen by the actor; `no-authority` is printed where the graph names no such seat, and nobody is promoted into the role.

**THE THREE FACTS STAY APART.** The register stores `delivered`, `confirmed` and `reported` as three distinct facts and never collapses them into one reassuring word:

| act | who | what it records | what it closes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `deliver` | the line's own holder | the deliverable exists, with dated evidence and a reference | nothing — it opens the independent test |
| `verify` | a seat **strictly above** the holder | the deliverable is there (`confirm`) or is not (`reject`) | one line's test |
| `report` | the accountable executive | the counterparty was told **in writing**, under a named instrument, on a day | the promise, once **every** line is confirmed |

**Independence is enforced, not documented.** `verify` re-proves independence inside the action with the same predicate the badge uses (`undertakingVerifierReach`): the tester must be strictly above the holder — never the holder, never below them, never an unrelated branch. The person who delivers does not grade. The badge and the action share **one** function so the register can never offer a seat a key the action then refuses.

**A report is refused until every line is confirmed.** `report` is refused (`not-every-line-is-confirmed`) while any line is untested, refused from anybody but the declaring executive (`not-the-declaring-executive`), refused twice, and refused if it predates the last confirmation on the record. **Doing the work is not telling them** — this is the substitution the register exists to prevent.

**A lapsed day is printed, never converted.** A due day that passes with no report reads `lapsed-unreported`, and `extend` is **refused** after the due day (`extension-refused-after-the-due-day`) — a passed day is not un-lapsed by moving the date; the counterparty already knows it was late. `extend` is also refused on an instrument whose date is **not the entity's alone to move** (licence condition, undertaking, representation, covenant, sub-certification) unless the counterparty's own written agreement is recorded on the record, refused if it does not move the day forward, and refused past the declared horizon.

**The precedence is declared once.** `UDT_STATES` orders the eight derived states, and `buildUndertakings` reads that order (`holds[key]` tested in the registry's sequence) rather than carrying a second hand-written ladder — so the kernel, the panel, the rule and the CSV cannot disagree about which fact wins. `lapsed` outranks everything (the counterparty experiences the lapse). `disputed` outranks a report. **`rejected` outranks `reported`** — a tested fact outranks the reassuring word. Where more than one test sits on a line, the **latest** test on or after the latest assertion governs: a later rejection is never hidden behind an earlier confirmation, and a test dated before the assertion tested a different deliverable and is reported (`staleTestCount`), never counted.

**RULE-UDT-01 (spine rule 150/150)** carries four conditions: `delivery-untested` (a line asserted and untested past the chase) asked of the **joint apex above the holder** — the lowest seat independence actually permits, so the holder is never asked to grade their own work; `report-owed` (every line confirmed, no report) asked of the **accountable executive**, because doing the work is not telling them; `due-lapsed` asked **once** per undertaking of the accountable executive; and `acknowledgement-owed` (a report with no reply past the chase) asked of the accountable executive. Asks are `act-inform` delegations with typed `issues-obligation` provenance, idempotent per (signature, addressee), self-closing when the condition clears or the addressee moves, bounded at 4 per commit with the remainder counted, and pause-governed on every path including the explicit scan (`undertakingAutoPropagate`). **The ask text names no counterparty, no instrument, no scope, no deliverable and no date** — a delegation is readable above the addressee and this register's subject is an outside party with enforcement powers.

**What it refuses to be.** No compliance score, no maturity rating, no league table, no "percentage of promises kept" per team, per line or per person. No undertaking inferred from a finding, a risk statement, a board memo, a contract record or a jurisdiction database. No automatic closure, report or extension. No document generation — the report act records the day and the reference; the register does not draft the letter. No jurisdiction lookup: the register stores declared terms and enforces internal consistency, and is not legal advice. A counterparty-kind count below the privacy floor of three exports **blank with its reason**, never a small number that would name a relationship.

## Verification

**One focused pass.** `node --test tests/app/undertaking-closure.test.mjs` — **22/22 PASS**: schema refusals and counted drops, holder-optional lines with no inference, act round-trip through the loader, `declared`/`in-progress` derivation, the three facts held apart across the whole lifecycle, a disputed report never reading as met, lapse never closing anything, the registry's precedence (lapse > disputed > rejected > reported), rejection → fresh assertion → confirmation in order, a pre-assertion test reported and not counted, the joint apex computed over two managers, `no-authority` naming nobody, partition-vs-figures reported apart, exact floor basis points over exact counts, empty-register blanks (never zeros), bucket coverage, instrument externality, unilateral extension printed not repaired, kernel purity, marker idempotence.

`node --test tests/app/undertaking-wiring.test.mjs` — **16/16 PASS**, and this is the display == stored == computed pass: the full matrixed lifecycle (declare → 3 deliveries → 3 independent tests → report) through the guarded API; **stored bytes re-read through the kernel and compared field-by-field with the panel row**; a report refused with two untested lines; the holder, a subordinate and an unrelated branch all refused as testers while the holder's own manager is accepted; an external instrument refused without the counterparty's agreement and accepted with it; an extension refused after the due day; a report refused from a non-declaring seat; a hand-edited malformed row and a ghost act dropped and counted; the seat-scoped attention count proved against the action that accepts it; `RULE-UDT-01` addressing the joint apex and **never naming the counterparty or the scope in the ask text**; the pause silencing every path including the scan; CSV #177 identity-free with every exported integer **compared against the kernel's count** and the rate blank-when-unsupported; and the jsdom DOM render printing the reader's own figures.

Four real defects were found by this pass and fixed at the source, none by weakening an assertion:
1. **The closed lists were compared as strings against arrays of `{key,label}` objects** — `UDT_COUNTERPARTY_KINDS.includes(...)` was always false, so every declaration was refused. Now `.some((row) => row.key === ...)`.
2. **The state precedence implied by the kernel disagreed with the one the registry declared** — a second hand-written ladder. The kernel now reads `UDT_STATE_KEYS` order directly.
3. **`in-progress` fired on a promise whose lines had no holder**, implying work was under way on a line nobody held. It now requires every line staffed; an unstaffed promise reads `declared`.
4. **The governing test was the FIRST test on or after the assertion**, which could hide a later rejection behind an earlier confirmation. It is now the LATEST.

A note on the fixture: the house seat model gives no operating seat to a person with no span, so obligation-line holders are **managers** — which is also what a licence condition actually lands on. The wiring fixture models that shape honestly rather than weakening the seat model.

## Consequences

- An executive can now answer, from the record: *what have we promised to whom, on which lines, on what day, and has anybody outside been told it is met?* — and the register will not print `reported-met` until every line has been independently tested and the counterparty has been told in writing.
- The counterparty is the beneficiary of the design: the register's second act, `report`, exists solely because "we did it" and "we told them" are different facts.
- The register does not verify that a cited instrument, correspondence or counterparty exists outside the workspace. References are declared, not checked, and the Method tab says so.
- Broader semantic absence beyond the word-boundary searches is `[UNVERIFIED]`.

The differentiators are structural, not decorative: three facts — delivered, independently confirmed, reported to the counterparty — held apart on the record with `reported-met` as the only state asserting all three; independence computed from the reporting graph rather than assigned by workflow, with the deliverer structurally barred from grading; the joint apex above **every** obligation line computed rather than chosen, and `no-authority` printed rather than an unrelated manager promoted; an external instrument's date not movable without the counterparty's own recorded agreement; a lapsed day printed and never converted; and a refusal-with-reason posture throughout, including a blank — never a zero — where a figure is unsupported.
