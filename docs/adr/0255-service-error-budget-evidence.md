# ADR-0255 — Reconcile a closed-window service error budget

**Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Status:** Accepted for the canonical source · **Surface:** `#/service-error-budget` · **Storage:** v207 → v208, one evidence ledger.

## Decision

A director or VP deciding how much change risk to take needs to know whether the customer-facing service met its objective, and whether the measurement is good enough to say so. The [incident command register](0240-major-incident-command-notification-clocks.md) records command and notifications, not every eligible customer event. [Measurement reliability](0069-measurement-reliability-terminal.md) grades a rating instrument; [Signal Integrity](0061-signal-integrity-board.md) spends statistical alpha. An incident count, a rating, and an alpha budget cannot substitute for a service error budget.

Use the [Google SRE error-budget policy](https://sre.google/workbook/error-budget-policy/) as the decision framework, not a hard-coded release policy. Give directors and VPs a closed-window source reconciliation they can preview, save and reopen. CEO review uses the same guarded evidence in reach. Frontline and mid-manager seats do not receive the portfolio telemetry in this slice. No approval, freeze, alert, or per-person score is inferred. The workspace does not know which release is planned or whether an exception applies.

## Evidence and exact calculation

- One workpaper declares a service reference, a versioned objective/query defining **good** and **eligible**, its exact target, an extract version, all accountable leadership stewards, and an inclusive **closed** UTC window of 1–90 days. No target, time frame or exclusions are inferred. A separately obtained whole-window control declares its own distinct reference and both counts for the same population. A distinct reference is not proof of source independence or completeness.
- Paste or open a local UTF-8 CSV/TSV (`day,good_events,total_events` in any order; exactly one row per represented day). Use disjoint, already deduplicated events across lines. No raw requests or customer identifiers are needed. Duplicate days, out-of-window days, impossible counts and malformed extracts refuse the entire paper. Missing days and readable mismatches can be stored, with the resulting verdict withheld. An observed no-traffic day must report `0,0` explicitly.
- Sum event counts, **not daily percentages**. Count strings stay exact in JSON and integer operations use `BigInt`; the target is integer parts per million (at most four fractional digits in percent). For `T` eligible events, `G` good and `p` good-target ppm, failures are `T − G`, whole allowance is `⌊T × (1,000,000 − p) / 1,000,000⌋`, and the signed difference is displayed as headroom or excess. Zero whole-event headroom is not necessarily exactly 1× burn. The success fraction is `G/T`; the observed burn multiple is the reduced fraction `(T − G) × 1,000,000 / (T × (1,000,000 − p))`. A 100% objective can be assessed, but its relative burn is undefined.
- The result needs readable coverage of **every day**, both control counts equal to the daily sums and at least one eligible event. Show recorded row totals and arithmetic balance apart from the control match and coverage. An observed zero denominator is not a 100% score. Print a reason for every withheld rate, allowance and verdict. A CSV numeric receipt uses the same result cells and blanks withheld numbers; no references, dates or identities leave in it. It is a numeric receipt, not a substitute for the on-screen provenance.

## Reach, durability and boundaries

- A declared director/VP/CEO must reach **each** named service steward, including any reached only by an active matrix reporting path. Reads and writes revalidate current reach. Preview does not save or retain a grant. A multipath steward appears once; no joint owner is inferred, and a seat reaching only one of several stewards cannot read the workpaper. The undeclared operator can inspect all papers and import warnings but cannot sign a new paper until a seat is declared. Seats are a local workflow disclosure contract, not authentication.
- `serviceBudgets` starts empty on upgrade. The workspace supports up to 100 papers; pasting/opening an extract is capped at 16 KiB. Persist only validated evidence, recorder, ID and capture instant. Identical source versions for the same service/objective/window are refused; on import all ambiguous copies are refused with a counted warning. Corrections append under a new version; nothing silently picks a winning paper or totals overlapping windows. An in-memory write after storage failure is explicitly not a durable save.
- The evidence ledger is report-only: no generic projection, grant, aggregate, or publication path serves its source data. The route has its own disclosure manifest. The recording act uses the existing event/store transaction with an opaque paper ID and no source text in the event. No new post-commit rule, badge, decision action or signal is warranted for historical evidence that may have been superseded. The normal full local backup retains the source ledger.
