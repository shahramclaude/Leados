# ADR-0241 — Workplace adjustment & functional-restriction register: a dated decision with a reason, an operational date the agreement is not, and a limit that is never cleared by silence

- **Status:** accepted and built — Schema v195, route `#/adjustments`, `RULE-WPA-01` on the shared spine, CSV scope #182, demo seed, insights signals (WPA-01..03), focused suite 34/34, standalone rebuilt.
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Deciders:** A restriction is **declared** against a written instrument, as a *functional limitation* — never a diagnosis — by a seat that holds the person. An adjustment has **two dates**, the day it was agreed and the day it became operational, and the register treats the gap between them as the record. A limit the workspace can prove is **computed exactly**; a limit it cannot prove is **refused with its printed reason** and is never rendered as "no conflict". A refusal with a recorded reason is a **defensible position**; a request nothing answered is a **defect**, and the two are never the same row.
- **Schema:** **v195** (v194 → v195). Five additive collections, all **empty at upgrade**: `wpaRestrictions`, `wpaAdjustments`, `wpaAcknowledgements`, `wpaReviews`, `wpaMarkers`. No restriction, adjustment, acknowledgement, review or limitation is inferred from anything an upgraded workspace already holds — a return-to-work plan on file is not a restriction somebody declared, and an absence of complaint is not a clearance. Six bounded conventions: `wpaAutoPropagate` (pause switch), `wpaAdjustmentGraceDays` 14 [0–365], `wpaReviewWarnDays` 14 [0–180], `wpaPrivacyFloor` 3 [2–20], and the declared night window `wpaNightStartMinute`/`wpaNightEndMinute` (1320/360), normalized on load so the window the register tests with is always the one on the record.
- **Surface:** `#/adjustments` — Register (every restriction, its derived state, its test against declared work, its per-line acknowledgement position), Declared work (the proven conflicts in exact minutes, and every untestable domain with the reason it could not be tested), Lines (each active line's acknowledgement gap), Method (the instrument set, the vocabulary and what the register refuses to do). Dialogs: declare restriction, withdraw, record adjustment, mark operational, record review, acknowledge line.
- **Code:** `workplaceAdjustmentRegistry.js` (closed vocabularies, WPA_RIGHTS, bounds, CSV columns/descriptor, method copy), `workplaceAdjustmentSchema.js` (fail-closed loaders, whole-minute limit parsing, night window normalization), `workplaceAdjustmentMath.js` (exact night minutes, span, merge, rest gaps, three-outcome limit tests, exact median), `workplaceAdjustment.js` (pure reader + derived state + insights signals), `workplaceAdjustmentActions.js` (seat-checked acts, all writes through `store.update`), `workplaceAdjustmentRules.js` (`RULE-WPA-01`), `workplaceAdjustmentReport.js` (CSV #182 rows), `views/adjustments.js` (panel). Wired through `schema.js`, `actions.js`, `main.js` (nav, badge, renderer, route aliases, help text), `accessGateway.js`, `seatRegistry.js`, `propagationContracts.js`/`postCommit.js`, `pausePermitRegistry.js`, `csv.js` (#182, group `people`), `insights.js`, `demo.js`.
- **Tests:** [`workplace-adjustment.test.mjs`](../../tests/app/workplace-adjustment.test.mjs) — **34/34 pass**.
- **Builds on:** ADR-0140/0141 (the ONE matrix engine — `matrixJointApex` decides who is asked, and is never re-implemented here), ADR-0209 (work & rest — this register **reads** `readEffectiveShifts` and never computes a second shift-minutes figure or a second time-zone resolver), ADR-0118/0159 (refusal with a printed reason), and the standing rules that a figure is derived on read, that an untested quantity is never printed as a passing one, and that a state which cannot be derived is not a state.

## Context

The "reasonable adjustments" record is one of the few HR obligations with a **statutory shape and no product shape**. Equality Act 2010 ss.20–21 (and ADA Title I on the US side) imposes a duty to make reasonable adjustments that arises **only on knowledge** — so the record of what the employer knew, when, and what it did is the whole evidentiary question. The practitioner literature is unusually blunt about where real organisations fail, and the failure is never "we had no policy":

1. **Agreed is not in place.** The category's own analysis puts it as *"the distance between those two dates is where the cost sits"* — an adjustment agreed in a meeting and never enacted is a live breach of a duty everybody believes is discharged. Almost no system carries both dates, because almost no system has a state for the gap.
2. **A decision with a reason, even when the answer is no.** *"A refusal with a recorded rationale is a defensible position; a request that was never answered is not."* Refused and unanswered look identical in a notes field and are opposite in a tribunal. A product that merges them destroys the only evidence that matters.
3. **The record must survive a handover.** The "Reasonable Adjustments Passport" idea exists precisely because the record does not travel with the person; it dies at the manager change. An acknowledgement **per line** is the minimum that makes the survival measurable rather than asserted.
4. **Never a diagnosis.** Occupational-health practice is functional-capacity reporting: record the *limitation the work must respect*, not the condition behind it. A register that stores a diagnosis has created the discrimination risk it was built to manage, so the field is refused at the loader **whole** rather than stripped.

Verified before building: no `wpa*` vocabulary existed in `app/` or `tests/`, `#/adjustments` was unregistered, and `RULE-WPA-01` did not exist. The nearest neighbours are `workRest` (which records *shifts worked*, and whose loader this register consumes rather than duplicates), `cases`, and the CSV scope name `workplace-adjustments`, previously unused.

## Decision

**A restriction is declared against a written instrument as a functional limitation; an adjustment carries two dates and the gap between them is the record; a limit is computed exactly or refused with its reason; and no silence is ever read as clearance.**

1. **Nothing is inferred, and a diagnosis is refused whole.** A restriction requires a person, a domain from the closed vocabulary, an instrument from the closed vocabulary, a bounded statement of the **limitation**, and a non-empty written **source reference**. A row that carries any diagnosis-shaped field is **dropped**, not scrubbed: keeping the row and deleting the field would leave the register printing a restriction whose stated basis it silently edited. Unknown domains, unknown instruments, an advisory instrument used as a prohibition, a review day before the declaration, and a number attached to a pure prohibition are each dropped with a counted warning. Nothing is repaired.

2. **Agreed is not in place, and the gap is printed exactly.** An adjustment carries `agreedOn` and, separately, `operationalOn`. `agreed-not-in-place` is its **own derived state**, with the exact integer days since agreement and the declared grace period printed beside it. An operational day **before** the agreement day is unreadable and the row is dropped. The agreed-to-operational median is computed only above its declared floor of 3 paired rows and is printed **with its halves** — `1.5` is printed as `1.5`, never rounded into a whole day that was never measured. Below the floor the figure is withheld with the reason, because a median over fewer rows is a proportion of nothing.

3. **A tested limit is not an untested one.** Three domains are testable against declared work — `no-night-work`, `max-shift-hours`, `min-rest-hours` — and every other domain is printed as `no-exposure-record` **with its reason**, never as clear. A lifting limit is never tested, because this product holds no declared record of lifting weights, and printing a 5 kg limit as "within" on the strength of a roster would be the exact failure that lets a real restriction read as cleared. An **advisory** instrument (`recommendation`) is recorded and never tested: treating advice as a prohibition would be the register inventing a duty nobody declared. Night minutes are computed exactly against the **declared, wrapping** night window; spans across two managers are **merged** before a minimum-rest limit is tested, so a zero-minute rest between two touching shifts is not manufactured by a line boundary. Limits carry **three outcomes** — `within`, `breached`, `unproven` — and an unmeasurable one is never `within`.

4. **The person is never scored and never asked.** A restriction belongs to a person, is carried by **every** active line, and is acknowledged **per line**; a person sitting on two lines owes two acknowledgements and the register prints the gap per line. The person carrying the restriction **cannot acknowledge their own line** — that is a self-signature, and the door refuses it. When `RULE-WPA-01` files an ask it addresses the **joint apex above the lines**, computed through `matrixJointApex`, and never the person. The ask text is **opaque**: no name, no domain, no minute count, because a delegation is readable above the seat it is sent to, and a restriction is a claim about a person's body.

5. **Declined is not unanswered, and silence is never clearance.** A recorded refusal with a written reason is its own state (`declined`) and is counted apart. It holds only while **nothing has been done**: a later adjustment on the same restriction outranks the refusal, so a recorded "no" can never shadow the action that followed it. There is deliberately **no `unanswered` state** — the workspace holds no request ledger behind it, and a state that cannot be derived must not be named. The absence of an adjustment reads `no-adjustment`, which is a defect printed as one.

6. **Time never lifts a restriction.** A restriction past its declared review day reads `review-overdue` at every later instant with nobody acting, and the door **refuses a withdrawal** that no recorded review decided — so a restriction cannot be quietly retired by a date passing, and cannot be retired before the review that decided it.

7. **`RULE-WPA-01` asks, once, pausably, and only the hand that can act.** Four conditions: a proven conflict with declared work, a restriction past its review date, an adjustment agreed past its grace with no operational day, and a live restriction on a line that never acknowledged it. One ask **per obligation** — per restriction for the first three, per (restriction, line) for the acknowledgement, because each line owes its own; collapsing them would hide one duty behind another. Signatures are opaque (`wpa1:work-conflict:<id>`, `wpa1:review-overdue:<id>`, `wpa1:agreed-not-operational:<id>`, `wpa1:line-unacknowledged:<id>:<managerId>`). Idempotent, self-closing when the condition clears, capped at four new asks per commit with the remainder **counted as deferred**, governed on every path by the pause switch, and never re-entered from an automation event. A row with no derived state is **skipped rather than guessed at**. Where an addressee genuinely cannot be resolved the rule writes an **identity-free observation** and asks nobody — a guard kept against a malformed graph, asserted in the suite as unreachable from any legal roster, and never printed as a state a reader could see.

8. **The export is a count, never a person.** CSV #182 emits one summary row and, above the privacy floor, per-domain and per-instrument aggregate rows. No person id, no name, no manager id, no statement, no source reference and no date crosses that boundary: a restriction is a claim about a body and the export is a number. A figure the reader cannot compute exports **blank with its reason**.

## Consequences

- A workspace upgraded to v195 gains a register that is **honestly empty**, with its conventions declared and its limits refusing until the leader declares them.
- The register's most valuable output is a **refusal**: `no-exposure-record` on a lifting limit is the register telling the truth that a roster cannot prove a physical limit either way. That refusal is an acceptance test, not a shortcoming.
- Two testable kernels (night window, hours) are proven against real declared work across **every** line, which is what makes the single-line case's blindness measurable rather than asserted.
- The demo seed carries four duties — two proven conflicts (420 minutes inside the night window; 540 against a declared 480), a line that was never shown a restriction it holds, an adjustment agreed fifteen days ago with no operational day — and a real median. Every figure on the panel is re-derived from the seeded rows by the reader; the seed states no state, no conflict and no duration.
- The register will not print a clearance for a domain it cannot measure. That is a permanent, deliberate limitation and the panel says so on its Method tab.
