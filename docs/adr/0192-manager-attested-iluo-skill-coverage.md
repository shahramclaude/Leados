# ADR-0192 — Manager-attested ILUO task coverage

Date: 2026-11-13 · Session 260 · Schema v152 · Status: accepted, bounded slice

## Duty and scope

Frontline / mid-manager primary; director, VP and CEO roll-up; single-chain and
matrix. A supervisor must know which declared tasks depend on a single qualified
person, which have no cover, and which qualifications have expired. This is the
coverage slice of Session 258's skills-and-versatility gap, not the entire TWI
training workflow. Frameworks: ILUO progression, TWI Job Instruction's observable
work standard, and reliability engineering's single-point-of-failure analysis.
The four-step TWI instructional workflow itself is not implemented.

## Record and authority

- `skillTasks`: one manager's task, observable standard, integer coverage floor
  (1–100), declaration timestamp and optional retirement day.
- `skillActs`: append-only I / L / U / O / revoked declarations, person, task,
  evidence, recording manager, inclusive expiry and `supersedesId`.
- A seat declares tasks only for itself. Only that task's manager may record
  evidence, and only for a distinct live person with a direct home OR declared
  matrix line to that manager. Ancestors, peers and the operator cannot
  impersonate that manager. The operator acts as the workspace leader.
- These are **manager attestations**, not independently checked credentials.
  A claimed O level does not prove an accredited trainer observed a test.
  Seats remain workflow declarations, not authentication.
- Declaring/retiring tasks and attesting/revoking qualifications use ordinary
  atomic `store.update` transactions with `skills.*` audit events and the existing
  post-commit pipeline. No new rule, setting, rule count or CSV scope is added.
  Autonomous training/reminder asks are deliberately not part of this slice.

## One reader; no hidden assumptions

`buildSkillCoverage` owns every displayed figure. It uses `buildMatrix`, including
its active lines and descendant sets; there is no second reporting engine. Each
task's pool is the DISTINCT current direct members of its declaring manager.
Qualifications are task-specific, never transferred to another line's task.
Former-pool attestations are retained and counted as excluded, not reassigned.

The explicit predecessor chain determines the latest declaration; ties in wall
clock do not pick a credential lexically. U/O count through the expiry day in
UTC; I/L, unknown, expired and revoked never count as independent qualification.
A floor of one can be met while still exposing a single point of skill. Empty
pools are printed as empty, never covered. History survives retirement, and
same-day retirement does not invalidate earlier legitimate attestations.

Roll-ups count distinct people and distinct qualified people across overlapping
tasks. Qualified task/person cells and people are separate quantities. Nested
span rows must not be summed. No availability, capacity, consent, station
assignment, simultaneous coverage or absence resilience is inferred.

The declaring manager and local operator see individual current evidence.
Ancestors receive task counts without person/evidence rows; below the ancestor
seat disclosure floor, qualification figures are null with a reason. A total
containing a withheld task is itself withheld, rather than exposing the task by
subtraction. The generic raw collection projection is not widened.

## Fail closed without reviving old evidence

Malformed rows, duplicate identities, broken or reordered predecessor chains,
invalid dates and forged manager/subject relationships retain invalid evidence
instead of dropping it and reviving an earlier U/O declaration. Any invalid row,
future-dated ledger evidence or canonical reporting-graph defect refuses all
coverage claims with a reason. Ledger bounds (500 tasks / 10,000 acts) refuse new
writes; an over-bound import retains excess rows in a stable recovery envelope
and refuses claims. Repeated normalization does not nest envelopes or lose the
rejected evidence. Repair currently requires a corrected workspace import;
there is no in-app repair editor or inferred repair.

## Interface and asset

`#/standard-work?panel=skills`: real task and qualification forms, explicit
retirement/revocation, current evidence, exposure, refusals and hierarchy roll-up.
The ordinary workspace JSON export/import carries the history; a dedicated CSV
or downloadable qualification dossier is not implemented.

`app/assets/skill-coverage.jpg` (39,046 bytes, 1568 × 518) is an AI-generated

## Newly discovered gap

**Skill-constrained simultaneous station coverage / Hall-deficiency witness.**
Two tasks may each show one qualified person while both rely on the SAME person
at the same time. The existing succession max-flow solver handles leadership
survivor eligibility, not simultaneous operating-station qualification. A future
slice needs declared concurrent station demands, dated availability/consent and
skill-qualified person edges, exact matching with a shortage witness, and matrix
joint-apex routing where lines compete. No inferred availability or automatic
assignment. Recorded in ROADMAP Planned; repository-wide absence beyond targeted
search is [UNVERIFIED].
