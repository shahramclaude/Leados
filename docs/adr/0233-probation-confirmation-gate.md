# ADR-0233 — Probation Confirmation Gate: the decision made and recorded before the window closes, on evidence from every declared line

- **Status:** accepted and **shipped** — domain kernel, actions, seat rights, `#/probation` route, CSV scope #176, schema v188 and the `RULE-PBG-01` spine rule are all wired on this branch.
- **Date:** 2026-11-16

## Context

Somebody was hired on a probationary period. The contract and the law give that period an end day, and the confirming manager must make a real decision before it — confirm, extend where the instrument allows, or end within the short notice the window carries. Then three things happen in most organisations and none of them is visible anywhere in the workspace:

1. **the day passes and nobody decides** — the default outcome (in most instruments automatic confirmation, or the loss of the short-notice lever) arrives silently, without a decision ever having been made;
2. **the manager decides on their own impression** — the project or functional manager who saw the daily work was never asked, so the evidence that mattered never reached the record; and
3. **the decision is made and never communicated** — the joiner is never told in writing, which in most instruments is the step that makes the decision real.

A lapsed probation is worse than a missed review: in most instruments the lapse **silently converts the terms of employment** — the short-notice lever is gone, whether or not anybody wanted it gone.

The repository had **no trace of the duty**. A grep over `app/` returned zero hits for `probation` before this ADR (the word appears only in this module's own files now), and the adjacency check confirms the hole: the workforce modules record starts, moves and exits, the delegation ledger moves **duties** between people, the goal and 1:1 machinery record ongoing management — but nothing records a **dated decision window** whose expiry changes what the workspace's own records mean, nothing puts an evidence duty on **more than one line**, and nothing treats "told in writing" as a recorded step rather than an email somewhere outside the system.

## Decision

**A gate is declared, not inferred.** The home manager declares: joiner, instrument (`statutory | collective-agreement | contract | internal-policy | assignment`), start day, end day, declared cap, whether the instrument allows extension. Declared spans outside 1–365 days are refused, declared caps outside 1–730 days are refused, and a declared span beyond the declared cap is refused with the numbers printed — the declaration is where the arithmetic is enforced, before a single act can land on the wrong footing. One open gate per joiner (`joiner-already-carries-an-open-gate`).

**The evidence duty runs on every declared reporting line — the matrix is the point.** A joiner on a home line and a project line owes their record evidence from **both**. The confirming manager asks each line's holder by id; the ask carries a due day (2–80 days out); the holder submits dated evidence with one of three closed recommendations — `support`, `concern`, `unresolved` — because "support" and "cannot say" are different facts and a register that let evidence read as prose would average them. Submissions are refused from anyone who is not the holder of the named line (`not-the-holder-of-this-line`), refused on lines that do not belong to the joiner (`line-does-not-belong-to-the-joiner`), refused twice on the same line (`line-already-asked`), and **refused for evidence dated before the gate's start day** — the window's own evidence floor, so a good word from before the probation began can never stand in for evidence from inside it.

**The restated home line is never a second duty.** Where the joiner's home line names the confirming manager as its holder, that line is the confirming manager's own impression and is **excluded** from the additional-evidence duty — `joinerMatrixLines(joinerId, day, confirmingManagerId)` takes the confirming manager's id and excludes relative to **that** seat, never the acting seat. Every call site passes it; the submit path, the decide path and the report all read the same three-argument predicate, so a gate never again counts its own home line as a missing second line.

**A confirm is refused without every live line's evidence.** `decide('confirm')` recomputes the joiner's **live** lines at the decision day (people move; the duty is judged against the structure that exists when the lever is pulled) and is refused (`confirmation-refused-lines-without-evidence`) while any live line lacks in-window evidence. Evidence whose line no longer exists on the reporting graph is **printed** as `declared-line-unresolved` on the row (`staleLineIds`, `counts.unresolvedLines`) and never silently repaired — and it does not satisfy the duty. A `concern` or `unresolved` from any line does not refuse the confirm — that is the manager's judgement to make — but the confirmation is **flagged** `confirmed-over-concern` / `confirmed-over-unresolved` for the rest of the record's life. An `end` needs no evidence: the urgent path stays short on purpose, because the short-notice lever only matters while it is cheap to pull.

**Extend is an act, not a decision.** Extension is refused when the instrument does not allow it (`instrument-does-not-allow-extension`), refused when it does not move the window forward (`extension-does-not-move-the-window-forward`), and refused when total span would exceed the declared cap (`extension-exceeds-the-declared-cap`) — with the arithmetic printed. It closes nothing: after an extension the evidence duty **runs again** against the new window (evidence must post-date the extension day), and the final decision is still owed. Decisions are two and only two: `confirm` and `end`.

**The window is a lever that disappears.** A decision dated after the effective end day is refused (`probation-window-has-closed`) — the gate reads `lapsed-no-decision`, the lapse is printed on every report and counted in the badge, and **no default outcome is ever applied**: the workspace does not confirm, does not end, does not assume. The record says the day passed and nobody decided, which is the fact a leader needs to see. Notification (`notify`) is refused before a decision exists, refused twice, refused when it pre-dates the decision; an unnotified decision keeps the gate in the attention count until the written record is on file.

**The attention count counts what a seat owes next, and nothing else:** a decision owed by this seat (their own gate, evidence complete or window closing or lapsed), an overdue ask owed by a line holder, an unnotified decision. On-time and within-window rates are **withheld whole** — not zeroed — when any gate is hidden from the seat by the privacy floor (rates over fewer than 3 gates print BLANK-with-reason in the CSV too), because a manager who can see only some of the gates must not read a rate computed from all of them.

**RULE-PBG-01 (spine rule 149/149)** carries four conditions: an ask past its due day with no evidence (to the line holder), a decision owed on a closing or lapsed gate (to the confirming manager), an unnotified decision (to the confirming manager), and a lapsed gate (to the confirming manager). Asks are opaque, identity in the typed link alone, marker-idempotent per `pbg1:` signature, bounded at 4 asks per commit with the remainder counted, self-closing on human closure, and pause-governed on every path including the explicit scan.

## Defects found by the focused verification pass, all fixed at the source

1. **The evidence exclusion was actor-relative, not declarer-relative.** `joinerMatrixLines` excluded the restated home line relative to the **acting** seat, so the project-line holder was refused submitting their own line's evidence ("line does not belong to the joiner"). The exclusion is now always relative to the confirming manager (`gate.declaredById`), and every call site passes it — decide's own evidence check had the same defect and read the restated home line as a missing duty.
2. **The candidate register printed nothing to the one seat that owns the duty.** `probationGateCandidates` matched joiners against the acting seat's id and then filtered lines through a reach predicate that returned nothing under a declared seat — the home manager saw an empty candidates list. It now matches joiners on the seat's own node id and scopes lines to those joiners, so the register offers exactly the direct reports a declaration can be made for.
3. **The two report reads threw before they could read.** `probationGateActions.js` called `buildProbationReport` and `probationGateAttentionCount` without importing them — every `probationGateReport` and attention read through the guarded API died with a `ReferenceError`. Caught by the wiring suite's first run; fixed at the import.
4. **The attention test misread the closing window — and the assertion was kept, the code re-checked.** The "decision owed" count fires inside the declared closing window (default 7 days) before the effective end day; the fixture placed the assertion 22 days out, which the reader correctly declined to count. The fixture was corrected and the closing-window arithmetic asserted explicitly on both sides of the boundary.

LeadOS **matches** the governance layer: dated windows, a recorded review, the written-notification step, deadline asks, an audit-trail export. It **exceeds** the four published descriptions on: refusal with a printed reason instead of a warning (cap breach, confirm-without-evidence, decide-after-window); the matrix evidence duty on every declared line rather than one manager's assessment; lapse printed and never defaulted (no vendor document claims to withhold the default outcome); and the identity-free CSV with privacy-floor withholding. It **does not match** and does not claim: country-specific legal rule libraries, payroll or contract-document generation, e-signature, questionnaire builders.

## Consequences

The gate never scores the joiner: evidence is one of three closed recommendations per line, never a rating, and the module's method tab says so in plain words. The register holds at most 200 gates and 4,000 acts (oldest acts beyond the cap are counted and refused rather than silently dropped), evidence text is bounded (24–600 characters), and every figure is re-derived on read. What it deliberately does not do: no HR machinery declaring gates (the duty belongs to the manager who answers for the decision), no inference of a gate from a start date, no capability score, no benchmark of joiners against each other. A gate on a joiner who leaves the roster prints `joiner-not-active` and is never repaired away. Seats remain workflow declarations, not authentication.
