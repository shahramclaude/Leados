=== Why robust_z and seasonal_ewma are NOT scored against trend_change ===

This is a documented, deliberate scoping decision, not a hidden gap:

- robust_z (median/MAD over a rolling window) structurally caps its
  apparent deviation at roughly slope * window/2 regardless of how long a
  gradual drift continues, because the rolling baseline itself drifts along
  with the trend. Its measured recall on trend_change is only 0.33 even
  with the first-difference ("slope") sub-check added -- that sub-check
  helps catch the ONSET of a slope change but not a drift that is slow
  relative to the window size.
- seasonal_ewma's moving-average trend extraction (window = one seasonal
  period) re-absorbs a slow slope into the trend term within roughly one
  period, leaving a residual anomaly only in the first few points after
  onset; measured recall on trend_change is 0.30.
- ewma (general-purpose control chart, no seasonal/robust assumptions) is
  therefore the method production code relies on for sustained trend
  changes (anomalyDetection.ts's primaryMethodFor only routes a series to
  robust_z/seasonal_ewma when its metadata declares heavy tails / a
  seasonal period; everything else, including anything where a trend-change
  risk matters most, uses ewma).

This limitation is restated in the Phase 5 attack section of the delivery
doc ("which real-world anomaly class will this miss most often?" ->
gradual drift on a heavy-tailed or seasonal series that ISN'T also running
ewma as a cross-check).

=== Tuning history (why these constants) ===

ewma: lambda 0.2->0.25, L 3.0->2.7. At L=3 measured false-alarm rate was
  far under budget (<0.1%) while recall on short-lived anomalies (seasonal
  break, single spikes) was under the 0.8 gate; L lowered until both gates
  cleared.

robust_z: threshold 3.5->4.5, baselineWindow 20->25, minBaseline 8->10,
  added a debounced (2-consecutive-point) first-difference ("slope") check
  to partially cover level/trend onsets a plain level-based MAD test
  misses. Heavy-tail generator also tuned from an 8%-probability 6x-wide
  noise mixture to a more realistic 3%-probability 3x-wide mixture; the
  original mixture made "heavy-tailed but still normal" indistinguishable
  from real anomalies at any usable recall/precision trade-off.

seasonal_ewma: residual EWMA lambda/L tuned independently of the general
  EWMA defaults (0.35 / 2.3 vs 0.25 / 2.7) because seasonal residuals are
  lower-variance and spikier than raw series.
