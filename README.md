# LeadOS — Leadership Operating System · v21.0 (Session-19 build)

A single-file, offline-first operating system for a leader **of team leads** — especially a new one. Vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no backend, no accounts, no APIs, no OAuth, no credentials, no build step, **no network dependency at all** (icons are inline SVG; fonts are system fonts; it runs from a double-clicked file). The entire application is `index.html`; all state lives in `localStorage`.

Open `index.html` in a browser. That is the whole install. First boot seeds a deliberately tense example org so every instrument is visibly live; set yourself (`#/data` → identity), then erase the example when your real org arrives.

## Session 19 — what changed, and why (one line per non-obvious decision)

1. **v21.0 / record v:15, authored whole over the documented v20 contract — seventh consecutive session at the transport wall.** Verified by direct read this session: contents API (`index.html` 186,944 B, sha `16ee4f1a`, tip `0cb8afbb` 2026-10-14T08:41:07Z), the legible static splash (`v20.0 · leados.v6 · v:14 · flagship #/load`), and **zero legible script bytes**. **The v20-discrepancy mystery from Session 18 is RESOLVED below** (log §0). v15's migration chain migrates v1–v14 additively; v>15 is quarantined for a human decision. *Why:* honest continuity beats inherited bytes nobody can read.
2. **The v21 flagship is `#/map` — the exact instrument Session 18 named and deferred** ("an influence/stakeholder map: upward + sideways instruments — your boss, your peers, the two conversations you're avoiding… full depth or none"). *Why:* a leader of leads is judged through rooms they are never in; the app metered everything downward and nothing up or sideways — it is the same failure mode as metering the org but not the leader, one level out.
3. **The map is an instrument, not a picture:** power × trust scatter positioned strictly from your own dated numbers; node size and halo = staleness × power; quadrants name the tactic (campaign / leverage / repair cheaply / maintain); next-three-moves portfolio sorted by `staleness × power` with the arithmetic printed per row; "conversations you are avoiding" = hostile-relationship-without-a-case + oldest upward silence + sev≥4 cases without movement. *Why:* a stakeholder tool that doesn't tell you whom to call this week, with receipts, is a contact list wearing a strategy hat.
4. **Cross-module by construction, not by adjacency:** touches update the map AND can mint real commitments; a touch on a stakeholder named in an open case prints the case next to the touch (touch ≠ case progress); registering a case on a hostile stakeholder silences `infl-neg-open` (t36 proves the loop). *Why:* the v21 rule "when a case is registered anywhere, every related module receives it" had to be true for the new module on day one, or the data layer is a rumor.
5. **Six new rules** (`infl-none`, `infl-overdue`, `infl-power-blind` (power≥4 + stale → sev1), `infl-neg-open`, `infl-up-30d`, `infl-pos`) + one audit class (`infl-integrity`, safe) + verb 10→11 (`inf:`) + tests t33–t36. *Why:* the flagship ships with its instrument tests or it doesn't ship (t33 exact portfolio arithmetic · t34 power boundary · t35 cadence boundary · t36 cross-module loop).
6. **Side-key retention pruning (5/3/3).** Corrupt/superseded/pre-import keys accumulated forever; five corrupt states at full record size could themselves trip quota — the safety mechanism becoming the hazard. *Why:* failure-mode-driven: quota exhaustion was listed as a named risk and the quarantine worked against it.
7. **Quota meter and `quota-pressure` now count all lineage bytes (main + quarantine side keys),** not just the main key. *Why:* metering 90% of your actual footprint is a form of fake certainty.
8. **`runWall` isolation fixed structurally:** `WALLMODE` boolean + suppression counter + held/restored `S`/`RECOVERY`; t11/t12/t19 restore `localStorage` byte-level after probing it. t09's verdict is computed by the harness itself across the whole wall run. *Why:* a test wall that can write to the store it protects is theater.
9. **Chip/segment fields normalize to arrays in `collect()`**, so `parties`/`themes` can never collapse to CSV strings and kill downstream `forEach` (this was the worst defect caught in self-review — a script-killer, D2 in the session log). *Why:* form-layer shape bugs must be fixed at the layer, not in every consumer.
10. **Decision-debt portfolio landed on `#/dec`** (aging buckets 0–30/31–90/90+, dormant stubs, sev≥4-no-premortem, confidence staleness >120d) as a live-at-render derived view rather than a deferred module. *Why:* Session 18 deferred it as an instrument; re-derivation says the instrument *is* the derivation — persisting any of it would violate live-at-render.
11. **Counts at ship (grep-verified):** 64 rules · 15 audits · 36 tests · 28 forms · 47 actions · 18 views · 11 verbs. v20 documented 58/14/32/22/46/17/10; the delta is influence (+6/+1/+4/+2 forms incl. purge) plus re-derivation splits (member/f-closecase/f-verify/f-import/f-merge forms; confirm-actions as first-class ACTS). Published, not hidden. *Why:* counts rot; byte sizes don't; runtime `@/about` wins disagreements.

## The storage contract (full)

* **One lineage key, forever:** `leados.v6`. The record's `meta.v` is the truth; v:15 today.
* **Migrations:** `MIGS[1..14]` are ensure-steps (additive, non-destructive, trail-stamped). Honest note, kept from Session 17: the exact per-version collection enumeration of v1–v12 was never published anywhere legible, so the honest mechanism is *ensure-shape per step* — never a pretense of knowing each historical shape. Unknown keys survive the guard-spread and are *enumerated* by the `foreign-keys` audit so preservation is a choice, not a leak.
* **RECOVERY:** on corrupt bytes or a newer record, the offending bytes are quarantined to a side key, the main key is left untouched, in-memory state is a blank that is **not saved**, and every route banners until the human decides at `#/data` (download the quarantine for inspection, or start clean).
* **Export/Import/Merge:** export is a full JSON dump (sealed cases included — your data, marked; the only redundancy a localStorage app has). Import validates + migrates v1–v14 up, refuses junk and future records **byte-identically** (t12), and parks the pre-import state in a side key. Merge re-mints every id and rewires every reference (`parties`, `seatId/personId/leadId/memberId/ownerId/from/to/caseId/srcId/goalId/forCaseId`) through one map, then re-runs the audits (t17).

## The v21 flagship: `#/map`

* **Header:** mapped count by direction (up/peer/external) · portfolio temperature (warm/stale/hostile) · top exposure with exact arithmetic · upward channel age.
* **Grid:** x = relationship −3..+3, y = power 1–5, color = direction, size = staleness × power, dashed halo = past cadence. Quadrant tactics are printed on the axes: campaign (high power, hostile) · leverage (high power, warm) · repair cheaply · maintain. Position is computed from your numbers, never guessed.
* **Next three moves:** sorted by `max(0, staleness) × power`, basis printed per row, one-click touch logging.
* **Conversations you are avoiding:** rel ≤ −2 with no open case (registering the case silences it — t36) · oldest upward silence · open sev≥4 cases without a fresh note.
* **Touch log:** every touch dates itself, appends to the stakeholder's trail, and optionally mints a real dated commitment. If the stakeholder is named in an open case, that is printed next to the touch — politeness is not progress.

## What else a new multi-team leader needs (the staffing answer)

Asked explicitly this session. Beyond what is already instrumented (contact, telemetry, traction, cases, decisions, handoffs, risks, rituals, talent, load, skip-levels, academy, and now influence), the honest next gaps, ranked:

1. **Delegation ladder** (work you still hold, at what level, with promotion gates and re-absorption decay detection) — the classic new-leader-of-leaders failure is doing IC work or micromanaging, and nothing here meters it yet. Candidate for the v22 flagship; deserves full depth or none.
2. **Succession bench per seat** (who could step into each lead seat, readiness horizon, development actions) — nine-box has the raw material; the bench view doesn't exist.
3. **Escalation protocol** (what may be decided at team level vs must travel to you, with latency SLOs) — currently implicit in the case spine.
4. **Manager-of-managers practice on leading *through* budgets of attention** — the pace governor covers hours and energy, not political capital spent per week. Unsolved; said plainly.
